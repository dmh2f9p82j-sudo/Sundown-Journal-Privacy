Privacy Policy for Sundown Journal

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Sundown Journal ("the app") is a personal journaling app. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Summary

Sundown Journal does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data with us. Everything you write — including any location you choose to add — stays on your device.

What data the app stores

The app stores the following information, entirely on your device:

Your journal entries (text you write in each of the six daily fields)
Photos you choose to attach to journal entries
Location information you choose to add to an entry (see "Location tagging" below)
Your daily reminder notification settings (whether it's on, and what time)
Your chosen visual theme and any prompt customizations

This data is saved using your device's local storage. It is never sent to us, to any server we operate, or to any third party.

Location tagging

Sundown Journal lets you optionally tag a journal entry with a location. This feature is entirely optional and off by default for any given entry — you choose whether to add a location each time you write.

There are two ways to add a location:

Type or search for a place name. This does not use your device's GPS or read your current location — it's a text search, similar to typing anything else into an entry.
Use your current location. If you choose this option, the app asks iOS for permission to access your device's location (only while the app is in use) and uses it to fill in a place name for that entry.

Either way, the location is saved only on your device, as part of that journal entry. It is never transmitted anywhere, never used for tracking, and never shared with us or anyone else. If you delete the entry, the location data is deleted with it.

What we don't do
We don't collect analytics or usage data
We don't use advertising or tracking technologies
We don't require an account, login, or email address
We don't share, sell, or transmit your journal content — including any location you add — to anyone
We don't track your location in the background or at any time outside of you actively choosing to tag an entry
We have no server, and no access to your entries under any circumstances
Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder feature, the app schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. This is handled entirely by iOS and does not involve sending any data off your device.

Photos

If you attach a photo to an entry, it is copied into the app's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere. If you delete an entry with a photo, the photo file is deleted from your device at the same time.

Printing and sharing

If you choose to print or share a range of your journal entries, the app generates that document on your device and hands it to iOS's standard share or print options (such as Mail, Files, AirDrop, or a printer). What you do with the document after that — who you send it to, whether you print it — is entirely your choice and outside the app's control. The app itself does not send this document anywhere on its own.

Data deletion

Because all data lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes all of your journal data permanently, including any locations or photos attached to entries. There is no copy stored anywhere else, so this cannot be undone — consider using your device's backup features (such as iCloud device backup) if you want a safety net.

Children's privacy

Sundown Journal does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data from anyone at all — everything stays on the device it was written on.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future — for example, if a feature like cloud sync is added — this page will be updated, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that change.
