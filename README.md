Privacy Policy for Sundown Journal

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Sundown Journal ("the app") is a personal journaling app. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Summary

Sundown Journal does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything you write stays on your device.

What data the app stores

The app stores the following information, entirely on your device:

Your journal entries (text you write in each of the six daily fields)
Photos you choose to attach to journal entries
Your daily reminder notification settings (whether it's on, and what time)

This data is saved using your device's local storage. It is never sent to us, to any server we operate, or to any third party.

What we don't do
We don't collect analytics or usage data
We don't use advertising or tracking technologies
We don't require an account, login, or email address
We don't share, sell, or transmit your journal content to anyone
We have no server, and no access to your entries under any circumstances
Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder feature, the app schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. This is handled entirely by iOS and does not involve sending any data off your device.

Photos

If you attach a photo to an entry, it is copied into the app's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere. If you delete an entry with a photo, the photo file is deleted from your device at the same time.

Data deletion

Because all data lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes all of your journal data permanently. There is no copy stored anywhere else, so this cannot be undone — consider using your device's backup features (such as iCloud device backup) if you want a safety net.

Children's privacy

Sundown Journal does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future — for example, if a feature like backup sync is added — this page will be updated, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that change.

Contact
