## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where is my data stored?**
Everything you write, any photos you attach, and any locations you tag stay on your device. Sundown Journal doesn't use accounts or cloud storage, so there's nothing stored on our servers.

**I deleted the app and lost my entries. Can I get them back?**
Unfortunately, no — since everything is stored only on your device, deleting the app deletes your journal data permanently. We recommend keeping your device's backup (like iCloud device backup) turned on as a safety net.

**Can I change the daily prompts?**
Yes. From Settings, tap into any prompt to choose from a curated set of alternatives, or write your own custom prompt entirely.

**How do I print or share my entries?**
From History, use the share/print icon to pick a date range. Sundown Journal will generate a document you can print, save, or share however you'd like.

**Does Sundown Journal track my location in the background?**
No. Location is entirely optional and only added to an entry if you choose to — either by typing a place name or by tapping to use your current location for that specific entry.
